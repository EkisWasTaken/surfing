Surf Sandbox

For Windows, free

CS-style surf for you and your friends, with a leaderboard for every map and a ghost of your best run to race.

Download for Windows

A 5 MB installer, for Windows 10 or 11. Or play in your browser instead.

Windows warning
The app isn't code-signed, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
Leaderboards
Get your group's code from whoever set it up, then enter it on the Leaderboard tab.
Have Counter-Strike?
The game finds it and uses the knives, skins and maps from your own copy. The files stay on your PC, you can turn this off on the Display tab, and the game plays fine without it.
Updates
The game shows an update button when a new version is ready.
